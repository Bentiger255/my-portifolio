Privacy notice

This notice explains what information may be processed when you visit Benjamin George Mbega’s personal portfolio and how to get in touch about it.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Information you provide

This portfolio does not currently have an account system, contact form, newsletter signup or on-site payment feature. If you choose to email me, your message and the contact details you include will be processed by your email provider and mine so I can read and respond to you.

Basic technical information

The hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser details, requested pages and timestamps, in line with its own policies. I do not currently run a first-party analytics tracker on this portfolio.

Third-party services and links

The site may load fonts from Google Fonts and project-preview images from a third-party screenshot service. Those services may receive technical request information when your browser loads their resources. Project links can take you to websites operated by other organisations, which have their own privacy practices. Review their notices before sharing information with them.

How information is used

Information sent to me is used to respond to your message, discuss a project or opportunity, and keep the site functioning safely. I do not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

Email correspondence may remain in email accounts for as long as needed to manage the conversation or meet applicable obligations. You can ask about personal information you have sent me, or request its deletion where appropriate, by emailing bentiger255@gmail.com.

Children and changes

This portfolio is a professional profile and is not designed to collect information from children. This notice may be updated if the site adds features or its data practices change.

Contact
